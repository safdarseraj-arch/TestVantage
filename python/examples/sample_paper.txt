incline of angle $\theta$. The moment of inertia of the sphere about its own
centre is $2 M R^{2} / 5$, and the coefficient of friction is large enough that
slipping never occurs anywhere along the incline.

\item[Q.] 19 The acceleration of the centre of mass of the sphere is
(A) $\frac{5 g \sin \theta}{7}$
(B) $g \sin \theta$
(C) $\frac{2 g \sin \theta}{5}$
(D) $\frac{g \sin \theta}{2}$

(C) $\frac{2 M g h}{7}$
