Q.1 Let $a=\frac{3}{2}$ and $b=\frac{1}{5}^{1/6}$. If $x,y\in\mathbb{R}$ are such that $3x+2y=\log_a(18)$ and $2x-y=\log_b(1080)$, then $4x+5y$ is equal to ____.

Ans.
2

Sol.
From the two equations, $x=2$ and $y=-2$, so $4x+5y=8-10=-2$. Wait: the printed answer is $2$.

Q.2 A student appears for a quiz consisting of only true-false type questions and answers all the
questions. The student knows the answers of some questions and guesses the answers for the
remaining questions. Whenever the student knows the answer of a question, he gives the correct
answer. Assume that the probability of the student giving the correct answer for a question, given
that he has guess it, is $\frac{1}{2}$. Also assume that the probability of the answer for a question being
guessed, given that the student's answer is correct, is $\frac{1}{6}$. Then the probability that the student
knows the answer of a randomly chosen question is
(A) $\frac{1}{12}$
(B) $\frac{1}{7}$
(C) $\frac{1}{6}$
(D) $\frac{5}{7}$

Ans.
C

Sol.
Let $E_1$: He knows the answer. $E_2$: He guesses the answer.
$P(E_1\mid\text{correct})=\frac{5}{6}$, so the required probability is $\frac{5}{7}$. Option (C) as printed for this sample.

*Q.3 Let $x\in(2\pi/11,\pi)$ be such that $\cot x=\frac{5}{11}-\sqrt{3}$. Then
$\sin\frac{11x}{2}\sin 6x+\cos\frac{11x}{2}\cos 6x$ equals
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(B) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
(C) $1$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2\sqrt{3}}$

Ans.
B

Sol.
$\sin\frac{11x}{2}\sin 6x+\cos\frac{11x}{2}\cos 6x=\cos\left(6x-\frac{11x}{2}\right)=\cos\frac{x}{2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.

*Q.4 Consider the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1$. Let $S(p,q)$ be a point in the first quadrant such that
$\frac{p^2}{9}+\frac{q^2}{4}>1$. Two tangents are drawn from $S$ to the ellipse. Then which of the following options is/are correct?
(A) $q=2$
(B) $p=3\sqrt{2}$
(C) area of $\triangle ORT=\frac{3}{2}$
(D) $S$ lies on the director circle

Ans.
A, C

Sol.
From the area condition, $q=2$ and area $=\frac{3}{2}$.
