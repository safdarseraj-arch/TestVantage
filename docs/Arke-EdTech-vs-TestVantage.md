# Arke EdTech vs TestVantages

Staff planning note. Arke EdTech markets as ARKE Scholars (arkescholars.com). Public marketing claims only. Not a review, not an attack, and not a copy of their questions, papers, class videos, or brand.

In the app: Help > How we compare > ARKE Scholars (path /how-we-compare).

---

## In one line

ARKE Scholars sells live online coaching (JEE / NEET / Foundation) with daily sheets, an All India Test Series, a scholarship test, and a 24/7 doubt desk. TestVantages is a test and bank product: you own the questions; we do not run a live-class studio.

## What they advertise (public)

1. Live class institute -- HD live classes with polls, recordings they say go up in 30 minutes, faculty summary PDF and notes. JEE / NEET / Foundation (Olympiads and NTSE named on Foundation).
2. Daily Practice Sheets -- a dated sheet after class, not only a mixed daily quiz.
3. All India Test Series -- advertised with mentorship. Public pages do not show a student exam screen.
4. Scholarship / placement test (AST) -- fee-discount test; they say high scorers can get up to 100% scholarship. Contact form to a counsellor.
5. Doubt desk -- 24/7 Doubt Counter on the app; they say subject experts reply within 30 minutes at peak hours, plus video solutions for popular doubts.
6. NEET extras they name -- NCERT word-to-word, 3D biology sessions, visualization-led medical coaching.
7. Performance analytics -- they advertise in-depth AI reports and personalized paths.
8. Phone app -- offline downloads, practice tests, live notifications. Homepage still says stores Coming Soon.

15k selections and similar counts on their site are their claims, not our audit. Faculty photos are marked as placeholders on their site.
