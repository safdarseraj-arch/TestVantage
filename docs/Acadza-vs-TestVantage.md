# Acadza vs TestVantages

Staff planning note. Facts from Acadza public pages and Play listing only. Not a review, not an attack, and not a copy of their papers, videos, or brand.

In the app: Help > How we compare > Acadza (path /how-we-compare).

---

## In one line

Acadza sells a packed JEE/NEET library plus classes and a phone app. TestVantages is a living bank and exam product: you own the questions, students practise and get analysis, staff convert and review.

## What they advertise (public)

1. Study material pack -- theory, examples, chapter summaries, a very large question count (Play listing also quotes a bigger number), about 9,000 formulas, about 4,500 lecture videos. Pick exam, subject, and concept.
2. Practice and mocks -- assignments and tests by chapter, concept, difficulty, and type. Unlimited mocks. Super-tough questions. Patterns they name: JEE Main, Advanced, BITSAT, NEET, CET.
3. Previous-year papers -- they advertise PYQ papers from 1975-2022.
4. Formula sheets -- build and download a custom sheet, with memory tips.
5. Backlog plan -- catch-up from missed chapters to current class, reminders and check tests.
6. Revision pack -- faculty pick five most probable questions for a topic (their claim).
7. Speed booster -- timed picking and clicking, plus racing friends or All-India rankers.
8. Doubt and mentor (paid add-on) -- Chanakya-style help; they say within 12 hours.
9. Rank and college from practice -- percentile, rank, and college guesses from test scores.
10. Classes and phone app -- live and recorded classes, class alerts, Android app.

Counts above are their claims, not our audit.

## What we have instead

- Prepare centre -- AI Mock Builder, Syllabus Practice, Precision Speed, CDF Mastery, Quick Revision, My Formula Sheet, Today's Target, Daily Practice Sheet, Placement check, Series calendar, PYQ Bank, official mocks.
- Question Bank (staff only) -- Kota-style bank you own. Convert OCR, Smart Compose, Gemini trial drafts, review queues. Students never see this tab.
- Question reports -- flag a bad item; staff mark it reviewed. Reports never delete the bank.
- PYQ Bank -- six modes, including a paper calendar (year to date/shift or Paper 1/2).
- Compete -- Today's Drill, Peer Pulse, Merit Stride, Study Circle, Ask Your Doubt.
- Insights -- My Results, Analytics, Form Forecast, Career Compass, Exam Pulse, Leaderboard.
- Teaching -- Lecture Planner, lecture sets, create/assign papers, School Management, teacher results.
