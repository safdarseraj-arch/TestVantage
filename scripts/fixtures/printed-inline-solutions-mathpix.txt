\begin{itemize}
\item[Q.] 1 A block of mass $2 \mathrm{~kg}$ rests on a smooth horizontal floor. A horizontal force of $10 \mathrm{~N}$ acts on the block. The acceleration of the block is
\end{itemize}
(A) $2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$
(B) $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$
(C) $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$
(D) $20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$

Ans. (B)
Sol. By Newton's second law the acceleration is $a=F / m=10 / 2=5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item[Q.] 2 A ball is thrown vertically upward with a speed of $20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. Taking $g=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$, the maximum height reached by the ball is
\end{itemize}
(A) $10 \mathrm{~m}$
(B) $20 \mathrm{~m}$
(C) $40 \mathrm{~m}$
(D) $80 \mathrm{~m}$ Ans. (B) Sol. At the top the speed is zero, so $h=u^{2} / 2 g=400 / 20=20 \mathrm{~m}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item[Q.] 3 Two resistors of $3 \Omega$ and $6 \Omega$ are joined in parallel across an ideal cell. The equivalent resistance in ohm is
\end{itemize}

Answer: 2
Solution
For resistors in parallel $\frac{1}{R}=\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{2}$, hence $R=2 \Omega$.

\begin{itemize}
\item[Q.] 4 The number of moles of oxygen atoms present in one mole of calcium carbonate is
\end{itemize}
(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) 4

Ans. (C)
Explanation: Each formula unit $\mathrm{CaCO}_{3}$ contains three oxygen atoms, so one mole holds 3 moles of oxygen atoms.
\begin{itemize}
\item[Q.] 5 Which one of the following gases turns lime water milky?
\end{itemize}
(A) Oxygen
(B) Nitrogen
(C) Carbon dioxide
(D) Hydrogen

Ans. (C)
Hint: Carbon dioxide forms insoluble calcium carbonate with lime water.
\begin{itemize}
\item[Q.] 6 The pH of a $0.001 \mathrm{M}$ solution of hydrochloric acid at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is
\end{itemize}

Ans. 3
Sol. The acid is fully ionised, so $\left[\mathrm{H}^{+}\right]=10^{-3}$ and $\mathrm{pH}=3$.
