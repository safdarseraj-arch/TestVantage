1. A block of mass $2 \mathrm{~kg}$ is placed on a rough horizontal surface with coefficient of friction 0.4. A horizontal force of $10 \mathrm{~N}$ acts on it. The acceleration of the block is

(B) $3 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$
(C) $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$
(D) $0$

Ans. (A)
Sol. Friction $f=\mu m g=0.4 \times 2 \times 10=8 \mathrm{~N}$
1. Find the friction first.
2. Then $a=\frac{10-8}{2}=1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$

3. Two identical charges of $5 \mu \mathrm{C}$ are kept $30 \mathrm{~cm}$ apart in air. The force between them is
(A) $1.5 \mathrm{~N}$
(B) $2.5 \mathrm{~N}$
(C) $3.5 \mathrm{~N}$
(D) $4.5 \mathrm{~N}$
Ans. (C)
Sol. $F=\frac{9 \times 10^{9} \times 25 \times 10^{-12}}{0.09}=2.5 \mathrm{~N}$

4. A wire of resistance $4 \Omega$ is stretched uniformly to twice its original length. Its new resistance is
(A) $2 \Omega$
(B) $4 \Omega$
(C) $8 \Omega$
(D) $16 \Omega$
Ans. (D)
Sol. Volume is constant, so $R \propto l^{2}$ and $R^{\prime}=4 \times 4=16 \Omega$
